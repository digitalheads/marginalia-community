## What is Marginalia?

Marginalia is a social reading app where you bring your own books. Upload DRM-free EPUBs, highlight passages, write notes, and see what other readers noticed in the same book.

- **Calm reading.** A distraction-free EPUB reader with Light, Sepia, Dark and Auto themes and text size, spacing and margin controls. The web reader has seven fonts, including Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and OpenDyslexic.
- **Highlights and notes.** Highlight in five colours and add a note. Each highlight stays private until you choose to make it public.
- **Social reading.** Follow other readers, see their public highlights in your feed, and explore public highlights from everyone.

Marginalia runs in your browser at [app.getmarginalia.app](https://app.getmarginalia.app), including on phones. It is made by [Digitalheads](https://digitalheads.pl), a small studio in Poland.

## Principles

- **Your books are yours.** You upload your own DRM-free EPUBs. Marginalia is a reader, not a bookstore.
- **Private by default.** Every highlight and note starts private, and so does your reading activity. Sharing is always your choice.
- **Open formats.** EPUB is an open W3C standard, and your files stay yours. A built-in export of your highlights and notes isn't ready yet. Until it is, [email us](https://getmarginalia.app/support#your-data) and we'll send you a copy of your data.
- **No ads, no tracking.** No advertising, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data. The website counts visits with Plausible, which uses no cookies.

## FAQ

**Is Marginalia open source?**
No. The discussions and bug reports here are public, but the application code is not. Marginalia is built on open-source projects such as epub.js, Readium and Lucide, and the app credits them on its [licences page](https://app.getmarginalia.app/licenses).

**What formats are supported?**
DRM-free EPUB 2 and EPUB 3, up to 95 MB per book. No PDF, no Kindle formats, no Adobe DRM. If your book is an `.epub` file without DRM, it should open. If it doesn't, please tell us (see below). [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) and [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) have free, DRM-free books.

**Is there an iPhone app?**
It's coming soon. In the meantime, the web app works in mobile browsers.

**How much does it cost?**
Marginalia is free today. A free account has a limit on the number of books and the storage they use, and the app tells you when you reach it. If we add paid plans later, we'll publish the price and terms before anything changes.

**Where can I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?**
See [Help and support](https://getmarginalia.app/support): it covers both, as well as reading offline, reporting and blocking.

**I found a rendering bug with a specific EPUB.**
Please [open an issue](https://github.com/digitalheads/marginalia-community/issues/new/choose). Include the book's title, where it came from, the chapter or section where the problem appears, and a screenshot if you can. Please don't attach the book file. EPUB rendering has a thousand edge cases, and every report helps.

## How we handle issues

We read new issues and discussions every working day. Bugs we can reproduce go into our internal tracker, and we label the issue so you can see where it stands. If we need more details, we'll ask in the issue.
