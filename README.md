## What is Marginalia?

Marginalia is a social reading app where you bring your own books. Upload DRM-free EPUBs, highlight passages, write margin notes, and discover what other readers are thinking — right where the text lives.

- **Beautiful reading** — a distraction-free EPUB reader with typography controls, multiple themes, and seven font choices including accessible options
- **Highlights & notes** — select text to highlight in five colors, add notes, keep them private or share with followers
- **Social reading** — follow other readers, see their public highlights in your feed, discover books through the people you trust

Marginalia is built by [Digitalheads](https://digitalheads.pl), a solo developer studio based in Poland.

## Principles

- **Your books are yours.** Upload your own DRM-free EPUBs. Marginalia is a reader, not a bookstore.
- **Private by default.** Every highlight, every note, every reading session defaults to private. Sharing is always an explicit choice.
- **Open formats.** EPUB is a W3C standard. Your highlights are exportable. No lock-in, ever.
- **No tracking.** No analytics cookies, no third-party trackers, no selling data.

## FAQ

**Is Marginalia open source?**
Not currently. The community discussions and bug reports are public, but the application code is proprietary. We use and support open-source tools (epub.js, Readium, Lucide, and many others) and acknowledge them in the app.

**What formats are supported?**
DRM-free EPUB 2 and EPUB 3. No PDF, no Kindle formats, no Adobe DRM. If your book is a `.epub` file without DRM, it works.

**Is there an iOS app?**
Coming soon. The web app works on mobile browsers in the meantime.

**How much does it cost?**
Marginalia has a free tier with core reading features. Marginalia Pro ($35/year) adds cloud sync, social features, and export capabilities.

**I found a rendering bug with a specific EPUB.**
Please [open an issue](https://github.com/digitalheads/marginalia-community/issues) with the book title, the chapter or section where the problem occurs, and a screenshot if possible. EPUB rendering has a thousand edge cases and every report helps.
